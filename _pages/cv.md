Summary
======

I did my PhD at *Centre Inria de l'Université de Lorraine*, in the
project team [CARAMBA](https://caramba.inria.fr/fr/), under the supervision of [Pierre-Jean
Spaenlehauer](https://members.loria.fr/PJSpaenlehauer/) and [Pierrick
Gaudry](https://members.loria.fr/PGaudry/).

I used to teach computer science from 2023 to 2026 at *Université de
Lorraine, Nancy*. I was also involved in the MathEnJean project
together with Paul Zimmermann in the same period.

I passed the competitive examination for the *Agrégation de
Mathématique*.
